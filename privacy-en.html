SaveGPS Privacy Policy

Effective: August 17, 2026 / Last updated: September 3, 2026

日本語

Yonagi Seisakusho (“we”, “us”) sets out below how information is handled in the Android application “SaveGPS” (the “App”).

1. Introduction

The App records your current location at the moment you choose to save it. It does not require an account, and it does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information stored on your device

When you perform a save, the App records the following in a database on your device.

  • Latitude and longitude
  • Location accuracy (margin of error in metres)
  • Date and time of the save
  • The address text obtained through reverse geocoding
  • Any note you enter
  • The lock (deletion protection) setting

This information is used solely to provide the features of the App. We do not transmit it to any server of ours, and we cannot view it.

3. Information sent to third parties

To provide the App’s features, information is sent externally in the following cases.

3-1. Obtaining addresses

To display the address of a recorded point, the App uses the reverse geocoding function provided by Android (Geocoder). Through this process, the latitude and longitude are sent to Google each time you save a location. The resulting address text is stored only on your device.

3-2. Obtaining your location

The App uses the location services of Google Play services to obtain your current location. In determining your position, that service may make use of information about nearby network environments.

3-3. Advertising

The free version of the App displays advertisements using Google’s advertising service, Google AdMob. For the delivery and measurement of advertising, AdMob may collect an advertising identifier, device information, IP address and similar data. Google’s handling of that information is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised advertising from your device settings at any time. No advertisements are shown if you purchase the paid (Pro) version.

3-4. Consent to advertising in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present a consent form before any advertising is loaded, and requests advertisements only in accordance with the choices you make there.

You can review or withdraw your choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the App. This item is shown only in regions where such a choice applies.

3-5. Purchases

Purchases of the paid version are processed through the Google Play billing system. Payment information is handled by Google, and we do not receive card numbers or other payment details. The App stores information on your device to determine whether a purchase exists.

4. Backups

The App supports Android’s automatic backup feature. As a result, your saved location history is stored in the backup area of your own Google Account, so that your history can be carried over when you change or reset your device.

This backup is held in your own Google Account and cannot be accessed by us. On Android 9 and later it is encrypted using your device screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) as the key. If you do not want backups, you can disable backup for individual apps in your device settings.

Information about purchase status is excluded from both cloud backup and direct device-to-device transfer.

5. Sharing that you initiate

The App can share a recorded point as text with other applications. This works only when you explicitly perform a share action, and you choose the destination application. What is included in the shared text (address, coordinates, note) can be changed in the settings screen. Once shared, the information is handled according to the terms of the receiving application or service.

6. Permissions used by the App

PermissionPurpose
Location (precise / approximate) To obtain and record your current location when you perform a save. The App does not collect location continuously in the background.
Internet access To obtain addresses and to deliver advertisements.

You may grant only “approximate location” instead of “precise location”. In that case the recorded position will be less accurate (roughly within 1 km). Permissions can be revoked at any time from your device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Recorded information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App
  • Individual records can be deleted by long-pressing to select them within the App
  • To remove all information, uninstall the App
  • For deletion of information held in your Google Account backup, follow the means provided by Google

8. Analytics

The App contains no usage analytics tools and no crash reporting tools.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change in response to changes in law or to new features in the App. If we make a significant change, we will announce it on this page.
